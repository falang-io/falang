<p>Text algorithms allow you to visualize algorithms in human language.</p>
<p>Currently, three types of schemes are available in text algorithms:</p>
<ul>
  <li><b>Function</b>: algorithm as a function</li>
  <li><b>Silhouette</b>: diagram of long-term processes. Analogue of the "silhouette" icon in the Dragon.</li>
  <li><b>Tree</b>: for describing tree structures</li>
</ul>

#### Rich text formatting
<p>Double-clicking a text icon opens a rich text editor with a formatting toolbar: bold, italic, underline, strikethrough, left/center/right/justify alignment, bullet and numbered lists, and undo/redo. You can also insert images — drag the resize handles on a selected image to change its size, and use the alignment buttons on its paragraph to center or right-align it.</p>
